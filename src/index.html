How to play

Goal

Six guardians hold the old ruins. Defeat each one, then light its beacon. When all six burn, the Gloam lifts.

Touch

Drag the left side to move; push the stick to its edge to sprint. Drag the right side to look. Tap the sword to attack, hold it for a heavy blow. Roll through attacks: you cannot be hurt mid-roll.

Keyboard

WASD move, mouse look (click to capture), left click attack (hold for heavy), Shift sprint, Space jump, Q roll, E interact, R flask, F torch, G firebomb, Tab gear, M map, Esc menu.

Controller

Left stick move, right stick look, X / Square attack, A / Cross jump, B / Circle roll, Y / Triangle interact, LB flask, RB firebomb, Back map, Start menu.

Dread

Darkness, the blight and wraiths fill your dread meter. High dread draws wraiths to you and, at its peak, wounds you. Carry a torch at night, stand near fire, or rest at a shrine.

Embers

Fallen foes give embers. Spend them at shrines to grow stronger. If you die, your embers stay where you fell; reach them before you die again.

Survival

Strike dead trees for wood and rocks for iron. Loot chests, camps and cairns. Craft torches, firebombs and armour by any fire. Climb watchtowers to chart the land around them.
