## 3. Where it feels like "a Pokémon mod for Minecraft"

- **No beginning and no end.** You get a Pokémon from a web button and are dropped in a Minecraft forest. No Oak, no rival, no badge, no reason to go anywhere except "levels go up with distance". Pokémon Red's progression (8 gyms gating routes, HMs gating terrain, rival checkpoints, Elite Four) is absent, so Red is reduced to its battle engine. That is exactly the Pixelmon shape.
- **Red is a hidden appliance.** Red's player stands on Route 1 forever; the world never becomes Kanto and Kanto never becomes the world. Evidence: the Pokédex AREA map shows Kanto nests and RED on Route 1 (`56b.png`); the music is Route 1's in every biome; a blackout sends Red's player to Pallet Town, which the page can't handle (B1).
- **Pokémon as mobs.** Wild Pokémon are billboard mobs placed by biome tables, with day "cute" and night "scary" casts. Scyther L3 in the starter forest at night. That's a mod's spawn table, not Red's routes.
- **Page-written people.** Nurse, Clerk, merchant and trainer lines are HTML toasts ("TRAINER: You beat me fair and square.", "NURSE: Your POKéMON are fighting fit…"), not Red's scripts. Red's NPC script engine is never used.
- **Two bags.** The Minecraft hotbar and Red's ITEM bag are separate; a POKé BALL bought at the Mart lives in Red's bag, a crafted one too (FROM CODE `givePoke`). The four Minecraft-items-in-battle (shears, wheat, bone, cod) are a small bridge.
- **Battles are a scene change.** Plants and leaves disappear (B5), trainers' fights look broken (B4), and the ball/shakes aren't drawn in the world during a catch: you watch Red's HUD, not a fight in a place.
- **What does read as a merge:** Red's actual battle code with world placement, trainer parties chosen by distance, ore/monster EXP feeding Red's level-up and evolution cutscene over the world, Red's money/bag/Mart, real stat/learnset rules. The rules are genuine; the structure around them is a mod's.

## 4. Top 5 recommendations (ranked by how far they move it toward a real merge)

1. **Gyms and badges built from the world, with Red's leaders and Red's gates.** Turn villages (or generated structures) into gyms with Red's eight leaders and parties in Red's order, and gate the world the way Red does: badges gate HM field use and level-scaled regions. Then there's a spine: a reason to travel, and Red's progression in Minecraft terrain. Effort: large (structure placement, leader battles, badge flags, gating rules), but it's what turns a mod into Red. The data is already there: open-pokered's `crates/pokered-data/trainers/` has Brock … Giovanni, Lorelei/Bruno/Agatha/Lance, Rival1–3, Rocket, ProfOak (checked the directory); `start_trainer_battle(class, index)` takes any class. Whether a leader battle started that way awards the badge (in Red the gym script does) I don't know.
2. **Fix the blackout soft-lock (B1) and the catch-as-faint bug (B2).** Blackout: handle Red's warp (PalletTown/last center) by warping back reliably or by resetting to Route 1 before checking, and show "You blacked out". Catch: map the capture outcome correctly. Effort: small; B1 is game-breaking today.
3. **HMs and TMs as Minecraft verbs.** CUT breaks leaves/grass, ROCK SMASH/STRENGTH breaks/pushes stone, SURF replaces "ride a swimmer", FLASH lights caves, FLY returns to a visited bed/village. Gate each on the matching badge, as Red does. Put TMs in Marts/chests. Effort: medium; this is the single best "Red's rules act on Minecraft's world" mechanic.
4. **Oak, the rival and a story frame in the world.** An Oak NPC at spawn who gives the starter through Red's own script (the lab sequence) and the Pokédex; BLUE appearing at milestones with Red's rival parties (they exist in pokered data); a goal (8 badges → an Elite Four structure). Effort: medium (Red's scripts exist; the work is placing them in the 3D world).
5. **Make Red's world-state match the world: routes, Centers, PC, music.** Replace the biome soup with Red's own encounter tables per "zone" (Red version only, Red levels, no Safari/trade Pokémon at L3), real Pokémon Center interiors or at least Red's Center script + PC (Bill's box) in villages, and switch Red's music by biome/time (forest → Viridian Forest, cave → Mt. Moon, town → Pallet/Viridian theme). Fix B4/B5 so battles happen visibly in the place. Effort: medium.
